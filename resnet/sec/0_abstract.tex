\textbf{Deeper neural networks} are more \textbf{difficult to train}.\ygm{(开篇直接点出核心问题:深度神经网络的训练是困难的)} \textbf{We present} [a residual learning framework] to \underline{ease} the training of networks \textbf{that} substantially\words{(显著地adv.)} deeper than those used previously.\ygm{(\ding{202}提出方法框架)} We explicitly reformulate the layers as learning residual functions with reference to the layer inputs, instead of learning unreferenced functions.\ygm{(\ding{203}解释核心机制:残差学习的本质不是让网络凭空学习目标函数，而是参照层的输入来学习残差函数)} We provide comprehensive empirical\words{(实证地adj.)} evidence showing that these residual networks are \underline{easier to optimize}, and can \underline{gain accuracy} from considerably\words{(显著地adv.)} increased depth.\ygm{(\ding{204}实验支撑并总结效果)} \textbf{On ImageNet dataset} we evaluate residual nets with a depth of up to 152 layers -- 8$\times$ deeper than VGG nets but still having lower complexity. An ensemble\words{(集成n.)} of these residual nets achieves 3.57\% error on ImageNet test set.\ygm{(\ding{205}展示效果)} This result won the 1st place on the ILSVRC 2015 classification task. We also present analysis on CIFAR-10 with 100 and 1000 layers.\ygm{(\ding{206}用比赛结果提高说服力)}

\ygm{(We present $\rightarrow$ We reformulate $\rightarrow$ We provide $\rightarrow$ We evaluate)}

The depth of representations\ygm{(网络层对输入数据逐步抽象后的特征表达)} is of central importance for many visual recognition tasks. Solely\words{(Only的高级用法)} due to our extremely deep representations, we obtain\words{(实现v.)} a 28\% relative improvement on the COCO object detection dataset. Deep residual nets are foundations of our submissions to ILSVRC \& COCO 2015 competitions, where we also won the 1st places on the tasks of ImageNet detection, ImageNet localization, COCO detection, and COCO segmentation.\ygm{(进一步论证了Deep Residual Net的泛化效果)}
